How it works

Three steps, no black box.

Every number on ModelXI traces back to real match data. Here's the whole pipeline.

01

The model learns

A Dixon-Coles model rates every team's attack and defence from thousands of real results across the last two seasons, weighting recent form more heavily.

02

It prices every market

From that goal model it derives calibrated probabilities for the result, handicaps, totals, both-teams-to-score, correct scores and more.

03

AI explains it

A language model turns those numbers into a plain-language preview — grounded strictly in the model's output and real form, never inventing a fact.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this betting advice?

No. ModelXI is statistical analysis for information — probability estimates and reasoning. It doesn't tell you to place a bet and it doesn't guarantee outcomes. What you do with it is your call.

Do you guarantee wins?

No, and anyone who does is lying. Football is uncertain by nature. We estimate probabilities as honestly as we can and show our reasoning; we don't sell certainty.

How accurate is it?

The model recovers real team strength from results and is built to be calibrated — when it says 60%, that should happen about 60% of the time over many matches. A public track record is coming so you can verify that yourself.

Which leagues are covered?

Twenty at launch — the big European leagues, the Champions and Europa League, plus Brazil, Argentina, MLS, Liga MX, Liga I and others. More as the model proves out.

Is it free?

Yes, for now. If premium tiers arrive later, the core analysis stays honest either way.
